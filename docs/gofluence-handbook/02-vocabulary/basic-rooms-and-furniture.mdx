# Basic rooms and furniture

## 1. Learning objective

Name the basic rooms of a house and the furniture usually found in each one, so you can describe your home in small talk or for remote work.

## 2. Key concepts

Each room has typical furniture. Learn them **together**: room + furniture.

| Room | Furniture and items |
|------|---------------------|
| Living room | coffee table, sofa / couch, chair |
| Dining room | dining table, sideboard / buffet |
| Kitchen | kitchen table, kitchen cabinets, kitchen island |
| Bathroom | sink, tub / shower, flush toilet |
| Bedroom | bed, side tables, cabinet / closet |

:::tip Two words, one thing
A slash means two words with the same meaning. `sofa` and `couch` are the same. `sideboard` and `buffet` are the same. `cabinet` and `closet` are close in meaning here: a place to keep things.
:::

:::note Use `there is` and `there are` to describe a room
- There **is** a sofa in the living room. (one thing)
- There **are** two chairs in the kitchen. (more than one thing)
:::

:::warning Countable nouns need an article
Say **a** sofa, **a** sink, **a** bed. For plural nouns, use a number or no article: two chairs, kitchen cabinets.
:::

:::danger Spanish speakers
`Closet` in English is usually a place for clothes, like an armario empotrado. A `cabinet` is a piece of furniture with doors and shelves. Also, `sink` can be in the kitchen or the bathroom: Spanish uses two words (fregadero, lavabo), English uses one.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| There is two chairs in the kitchen. | There **are** two chairs in the kitchen. | Plural noun, so use `are`. |
| I sleep in the **dining room**. | I sleep in the **bedroom**. | Dining room is for meals. |
| I put my clothes in the **kitchen cabinet**. | I put my clothes in the **closet**. | A closet is for clothes. |
| I have breakfast in the **living table**. | I have breakfast at the **dining table**. | The correct word is `dining table`. |
| There is sofa in my living room. | There is **a** sofa in my living room. | Singular countable nouns need `a` or `the`. |

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

Use: `sink`, `bed`, `sofa`, `kitchen island`, `dining table`.

1. I sleep in a big ______ in the bedroom.
2. We watch TV on the ______ in the living room.
3. There is a ______ in the bathroom for washing your hands.
4. We eat dinner at the ______ in the dining room.
5. I prepare coffee at the ______ in the kitchen.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Write the sentence with `there is` or `there are`.

1. Two chairs are in the living room.
2. Three cabinets are in the kitchen.
3. A tub is in the bathroom.

### C. Speaking prompts

- Describe the **living room** in your home. What furniture is there?
- Where do you work from home? What furniture do you use?
- Describe your **bedroom** in four sentences.

## 8. Quick recap

- Learn furniture together with its room.
- Living room: coffee table, sofa / couch, chair.
- Kitchen: kitchen table, cabinets, island. Bathroom: sink, tub / shower, toilet.
- Bedroom: bed, side tables, cabinet / closet. Dining room: dining table, sideboard / buffet.
- Use `there is` for one thing and `there are` for many.

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] I can name the five basic rooms.
- [ ] I can name two or three pieces of furniture for each room.
- [ ] I know that sofa and couch mean the same thing.
- [ ] I use `there is` and `there are` correctly.
- [ ] I can describe my own home in four sentences.
