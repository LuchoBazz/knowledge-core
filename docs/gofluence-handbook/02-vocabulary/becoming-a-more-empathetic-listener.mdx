# Becoming a more empathetic listener

:::info Assumptions
This is a vocabulary lesson. The twelve target words come from the class notes. The empathetic-listening phrases in section 3 and 4 are standard English phrases added to help you use the words in real conversations.
:::

## 1. Learning objective

Learn twelve useful words and phrases and use them, with simple listening phrases, to show empathy in workplace conversations.

## 2. Key concepts

**Empathy** means understanding how another person feels. An **empathetic listener** listens to understand, not only to answer.

| Word or phrase | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| come naturally | happen easily and without effort |
| undisputed | accepted or recognized by everyone |
| flawed | having a mistake or weakness |
| biases | opinions or ideas that can lead to unfair judgment of a group or idea |
| get to the bottom of | discover and understand the real reason or truth |
| engage in | participate or be part of |
| common ground | shared interests or opinions among different parties |
| disclose | reveal or make known |
| deepens | makes something more serious or more strongly felt |
| reframing | changing the way something is done or considered |
| tap into | access and use a resource to your advantage |
| audit | an official and thorough examination |

:::tip Collocations to remember
- `come naturally` **to** someone: *Leading a team comes naturally to her.*
- `engage in` + noun or `-ing`: *engage in a conversation*, *engage in learning*.
- `find common ground` **with** someone.
- `get to the bottom of` + a problem: *get to the bottom of the issue*.
:::

:::note Word forms
- `bias` is singular; `biases` is plural. *Everyone has a bias.* *We must check our biases.*
- `disclose` is a verb; `disclosure` is the noun.
- `deepens` is the third person of `deepen`. Add -s when the subject is singular: *Listening deepens trust.*
:::

:::warning `audit` is a noun, and also a verb
In the lesson, `audit` is a noun (*a safety audit*). You can also use it as a verb: *We audit our accounts every year.*
:::

:::danger Spanish speakers
`Disclose` does not mean "disculpar" or "descubrir". It means to **reveal** information (*divulgar*). `Biases` means **sesgos** or **prejuicios**. And `engage in` means to take part in an activity. It does not mean "comprometerse a algo" in every case.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Sales comes natural to her. | Sales **comes naturally** to her. | Use the adverb `naturally`. |
| We need to find a common ground. | We need to find **common ground**. | `Ground` is uncountable here, so no `a`. |
| The team disclosed about the results. | The team **disclosed** the results. | `Disclose` takes a direct object, no `about`. |
| I engage with weekly meetings. | I **engage in** weekly meetings. | Use `engage in` for taking part in an activity. |
| He has many bias. | He has many **biases**. | Plural of `bias` is `biases`. |

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

Use: `come naturally`, `flawed`, `common ground`, `tap into`, `audit`.

1. Public speaking does not ______ to me, so I practice a lot.
2. The report was ______ because the data was incomplete.
3. After a long talk, we found ______ on the budget.
4. We can ______ the experience of our senior engineers.
5. The company will do a security ______ next month.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Rewrite each sentence with the word in brackets.

1. We must find the real reason for the delay. (get to the bottom of)
2. Teamwork happens easily for Sofia. (come naturally)
3. Let us look at the problem in a new way. (reframing)

### C. Speaking prompts

- Describe something that **comes naturally** to you at work.
- Use three empathetic-listening phrases to answer a colleague who says: "I am overwhelmed with work."
- Talk about a time you found **common ground** with a colleague.

## 8. Quick recap

- An empathetic listener listens to understand, not only to reply.
- `come naturally`, `undisputed`, `flawed`: describe skills, facts and problems.
- `get to the bottom of`, `engage in`, `common ground`: understand and connect with people.
- `disclose`, `deepens`, `reframing`, `tap into`, `audit`: share, grow and improve.
- Reflect feelings: *It sounds like you are feeling...*
