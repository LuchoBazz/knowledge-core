# Computer and online verbs

## 1. Learning objective

Use common verbs for computers and online activities to talk about saving data, sharing information, keeping it secure and updating programs.

## 2. Key concepts

Group the verbs by **action**. This makes them easier to remember.

| Group | Verbs |
|-------|-------|
| Saving data | back up, retrieve, store |
| Sharing information | access, connect, share |
| Keeping information secure | block, filter out, opt in / opt out, protect, scan |
| Updating programs and devices | install, update |

:::tip Verb or noun? Watch the spelling
- `back up` is a **verb** (two words): *Please back up your files.*
- `backup` is a **noun or adjective** (one word): *We have a backup. It is a backup file.*

The same rule works for `log in` (verb) and `login` (noun).
:::

:::note Phrasal verbs
`back up`, `filter out`, `opt in` and `opt out` are phrasal verbs. Keep the two parts together. With a pronoun, put it in the middle: *back them up*, *filter them out*.
:::

:::warning `opt in` and `opt out` are opposites
- **Opt in** = choose to take part (*Opt in to receive our newsletter.*)
- **Opt out** = choose not to take part, or stop (*You can opt out at any time.*)

Use `to` after `opt in` for a service: *opt in to notifications*. Use `of` or no word after `opt out`: *opt out of emails*.
:::

:::danger Spanish speakers
`Retrieve` is not "retirar". It means to **get back** data that is saved. Also, `access` can be a verb in English: *access the system*. Do not say "enter in the system" or "access to the system" when you use it as a verb. Say **access the system**.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Please **backup** your files today. | Please **back up** your files today. | The verb has two words. `Backup` is the noun. |
| I cannot **access to** the system. | I cannot **access** the system. | `Access` is a transitive verb here. No preposition. |
| Please **update** to the file. | Please **update** the file. | `Update` takes a direct object. |
| Customers can **opt out** receive emails. | Customers can **opt out of** receiving emails. | Use `of` + verb with `-ing`. |
| Please share **to me** the file. | Please share the file **with me**. | Use `share something with someone`. |

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

Use: `back up`, `retrieve`, `block`, `opt out`, `install`.

1. IT will ______ the deleted file from the server.
2. We ______ our data every night.
3. The firewall can ______ dangerous websites.
4. You can ______ of marketing emails at any time.
5. Please ______ the new software today.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Correct each sentence.

1. Please backup your laptop before the update.
2. I cannot access to the shared folder.
3. You can opt out receive our newsletter.

### C. Speaking prompts

- Explain how your company **protects** its data. Use three verbs from this lesson.
- Describe the last time you **updated** or **installed** something at work.
- What do you **back up** and **store**, and where?

## 8. Quick recap

- Saving data: back up, retrieve, store.
- Sharing information: access, connect, share.
- Keeping information secure: block, filter out, opt in / opt out, protect, scan.
- Updating: install, update.
- `back up` is the verb. `backup` is the noun.
