# How to ask for and provide technical assistance

## 1. Learning objective

Report a technical problem clearly, ask for help politely, and give solutions and step-by-step instructions as a support person.

## 2. Key concepts

There are two roles: the **user** who has a problem, and the **helper** who solves it.

### The user: report and ask

```text title="To report a problem"
My ... is / are slow / erratic / problematic.
I'm having a problem with ...
When I click (on) / press / type ..., ...
The error message says, "..."
I've (already) tried ..., but ...
I've (already) tried ..., but to no avail.
```

```text title="To ask for assistance"
What shall / should I do?
How do / can I ...?
Can / Could you tell / teach me how to ...?
```

:::tip Be specific
Say **what you did** and **what happened**. This helps the support person find the problem faster: *When I click on Save, the app freezes.*
:::

:::note `to no avail`
`but to no avail` means *but it did not work*. It is a formal way to say your attempt failed.
:::

### The helper: acknowledge, then solve

```text title="To acknowledge the problem"
I apologize for the inconvenience.
It seems ...
I'd be happy to help / assist you with ...
```

```text title="To give solutions and instructions"
Have you tried ...?
Can / Could you please try ...?
Please / Kindly ..., and / then ...
You can ... by ...
You'll need to ...
```

| Function | Structure | Example |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Suggest | Have you tried + -ing? | Have you tried restarting the app? |
| Request | Could you please + base verb? | Could you please clear your cache? |
| Sequence | Please ..., and then ... | Please log out, and then log in again. |
| Method | You can + verb + by + -ing | You can fix it by updating the app. |

:::warning Politeness
`Could you please ...?` and `Kindly ...` are more polite than a direct command. Use them with customers.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| I have a problem in my computer. | I have a problem **with** my computer. | Use `with` for a problem with a thing. |
| I tried restart it, but it failed. | I **tried restarting** it, but it failed. | `try` + -ing for an experiment. |
| Could you please to send the file? | Could you please **send** the file? | Base verb after `could you please`. |
| You can fix it by update the app. | You can fix it by **updating** the app. | -ing after `by`. |
| I apologize by the inconvenience. | I apologize **for** the inconvenience. | `apologize for`. |

:::danger Spanish speakers
Do not say *I have a problem in my laptop* (*problema en mi laptop*). Say `with`. Also, `I apologize for the inconvenience` is a formal phrase. Do not translate it word by word as *me disculpo por la inconveniencia*.
:::

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

1. I'm having a problem ______ my printer.
2. The error message ______, "File not found."
3. I've already tried restarting it, but ______ ______ ______.
4. Have you tried ______ (clear) your cache?

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Make each sentence more polite.

1. Send me a screenshot.
2. Update the app.

### C. Speaking prompts

- Role-play: you report a problem with your email. Say what you did and what happened.
- Role-play: you are the help desk. Acknowledge the problem and give three instructions.

## 8. Quick recap

- Report: `I'm having a problem with ...`, `The error message says ...`, `I've already tried ..., but ...`.
- Ask: `What should I do?`, `How can I ...?`.
- Acknowledge: `I apologize for the inconvenience`, `I'd be happy to help you with ...`.
- Instruct: `Have you tried ...?`, `Could you please ...?`, `You'll need to ...`.

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] I can report a problem with what I did and what happened.
- [ ] I can ask for help politely.
- [ ] I can acknowledge a problem as a support person.
- [ ] I can give instructions with `Please ..., and then ...`.
- [ ] I use -ing after `by` and after `Have you tried`.
