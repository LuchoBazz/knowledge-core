# How to describe a product

## 1. Learning objective

Describe a product's features and functions clearly. Use feature phrases, `made of` / `made from`, causative verbs and compound adjectives.

## 2. Key concepts

### Phrases for features and functions

```text title="Structures"
The product has (feature/s) which (function/s).
The product, with its (feature/s), (function/s).
It can (function).
```

- The camera has an improved lens which captures sharper pictures.
- The app, with its simple menu, saves users a lot of time.
- It can translate messages in real time.

### Made of vs made from

Both describe how a product is created. The difference is about the material.

| Phrase | Use it when... | Example |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| made **of** | the material does **not** change. You can still see it. | This desk is made of wood. |
| made **from** | the material **changes** into something new. | Paper is made from wood. |

:::tip Quick test
Can you still recognize the material? Use `of`. Is it now a different thing? Use `from`.
:::

### Causative verbs for functions

Causative verbs show what a product **allows or causes** you to do: `let`, `make`, `have`, `get` and `help`.

| Verb | Pattern | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| let | let + person + base verb | It has an improved lens which **lets you take** sharper pictures. |
| make | make + thing + base verb | Its compact design **makes it fit** into a small bag. |
| help | help + person + (to) + verb | It **helps you clean** your house without harsh smells. |
| have | have + person + base verb | The app **has users confirm** their email first. |
| get | get + person + **to** + verb | The reminders **get you to finish** tasks on time. |

:::warning `get` needs `to`
After `let`, `make` and `have`, use the base verb. After `get`, use `to + verb`. After `help`, `to` is optional.
:::

### Compound adjectives

A compound adjective joins two words with a hyphen. It comes **before** a noun.

- point-and-shoot cameras
- germ-killing ingredients
- mild-smelling bleach

:::note Singular only
The first part never takes a plural `-s`. We say a **ten-minute** demo, not a ten-minutes demo.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Paper is made of wood. | Paper is made **from** wood. | The wood changes into paper. |
| The app lets you to share files. | The app lets you **share** files. | No `to` after `let`. |
| It makes you to save time. | It makes you **save** time. | No `to` after `make`. |
| a ten-minutes video | a **ten-minute** video | A compound adjective stays singular. |
| It helps you cleaning. | It helps you **clean** (or to clean). | `help` + base verb or `to` + verb. |

:::danger Spanish speakers
In Spanish we say *hace que puedas hacer*, *te permite hacer*. In English, do not add `to` or `that` after `let` and `make`: *It lets you work offline.*
:::

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

1. This table is ______ (made of / made from) steel.
2. Cheese is ______ (made of / made from) milk.
3. The app lets you ______ (create) reports quickly.
4. Its bright screen makes it ______ (be) easy to read outside.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Rewrite each sentence with a compound adjective or a causative verb.

1. The soap has ingredients that kill germs.
2. The new design allows the phone to fit in a small pocket. (use `makes`)

### C. Speaking prompts

- Describe a product you use every day. Use `has ... which ...` and one causative verb.
- What is your phone made of? Use `made of` and `made from` correctly.

## 8. Quick recap

- Use `The product has ... which ...` and `It can ...` to describe features.
- `made of` = the material stays the same. `made from` = the material changes.
- `let`, `make`, `have` + base verb. `get` + `to` + verb. `help` + (to) + verb.
- Compound adjectives use hyphens and stay singular: *germ-killing*, *mild-smelling*.

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] I can describe a product with `has ... which ...`.
- [ ] I know when to use `made of` and `made from`.
- [ ] I can use `lets you` and `makes it` correctly.
- [ ] I can create two compound adjectives.
- [ ] I can present a product aloud in three sentences.
