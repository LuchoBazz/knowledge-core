# How AI is shaping today's customer support

:::info Assumptions
The class notes contain only a vocabulary list with definitions and examples for this lesson. The grammar notes and workplace examples below are standard English added to help you use the words.
:::

## 1. Learning objective

Learn and use five key words to talk about AI in customer support: `agents`, `virtual assistants`, `transforming`, `personal touch` and `alongside`.

## 2. Key concepts

AI is changing how companies help their customers. Today, people and AI tools often work **together**. These five words help you talk about this change.

| Word | Meaning | Example from the lesson |
|------|---------|-------------------------|
| agents | employees who help customers with questions and problems | Travel agents give tourists helpful information about hotels and flights. |
| virtual assistants | AI-powered tools that help users by answering questions and completing tasks | The virtual assistants in M&P's mobile phone models can translate messages and suggest replies. |
| transforming | changing something completely to make it better | The fresh paint is transforming the plain house into a bright place. |
| personal touch | special care or attention that makes someone feel important | Isa wrote a note to add a personal touch to the gift. |
| alongside | together with something or someone | A nurse and a doctor work alongside each other to care for patients in the clinic. |

:::tip Word partners
- `transform ... into ...`: AI is transforming support into a faster service.
- `add a personal touch`: Agents add a personal touch to every reply.
- `work alongside`: Agents work alongside virtual assistants.
:::

:::note Agent in different contexts
In customer support, an `agent` is a person on the support team. A travel agent or a real estate agent also helps customers, but in other industries.
:::

:::warning `transforming` is the -ing form
Use `is / are transforming` for a change that is happening now. For a finished change, use `transformed`: *AI has transformed support.*
:::

:::warning `alongside` is one word
Write `alongside`, not *along side*. It is followed by a noun: `alongside agents`, `alongside our team`.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Agents work along side the assistant. | Agents work **alongside** the assistant. | `alongside` is one word. |
| AI transform our support. | AI **is transforming** our support. | Use the right verb form for a change in progress. |
| We add a personal touch for each reply. | We add a personal touch **to** each reply. | `add ... to ...`. |
| The virtual assistant are fast. | The virtual assistant **is** fast. | Singular subject, singular verb. |
| Virtual assistant can answer questions. | **A** virtual assistant can answer questions. (or **Virtual assistants** can ...) | Use an article or a plural. |

:::danger Spanish speakers
Do not translate `agent` as *agente* in every case. In customer support it means the person who helps the customer. Also, watch the false friend `assist`: it means *ayudar*, not *asistir a una reunión* (that is `attend`).
:::

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

Use: agents, virtual assistants, transforming, personal touch, alongside.

1. Human ______ answer the difficult questions.
2. The ______ can translate messages and suggest replies.
3. AI is ______ the way companies help their customers.
4. A short handwritten note adds a ______ to the gift.
5. Our team works ______ the chatbot.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

1. AI changes our support completely. (use `is transforming`)
2. The agents and the tool work together. (use `alongside`)

### C. Speaking prompts

- How does your company use AI in customer support? Use at least three of the new words.
- Give an example of a personal touch in customer service.

## 8. Quick recap

- `agents` = employees who help customers.
- `virtual assistants` = AI tools that answer questions and complete tasks.
- `transforming` = changing something completely to make it better.
- `personal touch` = special care that makes someone feel important.
- `alongside` = together with.
