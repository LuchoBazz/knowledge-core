# How to respond to feedback from clients

## 1. Learning objective

Respond to negative feedback from a client in a calm, professional way. Apologize, show empathy, explain the solution and keep a friendly tone.

## 2. Key concepts

A good response has several steps. You do not need all of them every time, but the order helps.

1. Apologize.
2. Clarify the problem.
3. Show empathy.
4. Take responsibility.
5. Explain how and when you will solve it.
6. Offer an alternative.
7. Summarize.
8. Promise a follow-up.
9. End with a friendly tone.

### The phrases, step by step

| Step | Useful phrases |
|------|----------------|
| Apologize | First of all, I'd like to say how sorry I am. / I'm so (very, really, terribly) sorry about that. / I apologize for ... / Let me apologize for ... |
| Clarify | If I understand you correctly, ... / Could you please tell me exactly what happened? / Could you explain ... a little more? / Would you mind if I just check ... again? |
| Show empathy | I can see why ... / I understand. / I see what you mean. / I would feel the same way. / What a difficult situation this puts you in. |
| Take responsibility | It looks like an oversight on our part. / There seems to be a misunderstanding. / It appears ... got overlooked. / I'm afraid there has been some sort of mix-up. |
| Explain the solution | I'll take care of this at once for you. / I'm sure we can find a solution. / This should be resolved by the end of ... / You'll receive ... by ... |
| Offer an alternative | May I offer ... as an alternative for ...? / I'll look into other possible ways of ... / Perhaps you could consider ... as another option. |
| Summarize | What we have decided is ... / Our action plan is ... / I'd like to review this once more to make sure we agree. |
| Follow up | I'll get back to you ... / I'll make sure that ... |
| End friendly | Thanks for bringing this to our attention. / Is there anything else I can help you with today? |

:::tip Clarify before you promise
Repeat the problem in your own words: *If I understand you correctly, the invoice shows the wrong amount.* This shows you are listening.
:::

:::note Softening responsibility
Phrases like `There seems to be a misunderstanding` and `It looks like an oversight` are soft and polite. Use them to accept the issue without blaming a person.
:::

:::warning Apologize with `for`
Use `apologize for` + noun or -ing: *I apologize for the delay.* / *I apologize for sending the wrong file.*
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| I apologize by the delay. | I apologize **for** the delay. | `apologize for`. |
| Can you explain me what happened? | Could you tell **me** exactly what happened? / Could you explain **to me**... | `explain` needs `to` before a person. |
| It was your fault. | There seems to be a misunderstanding. | Avoid blame. Use a polite, neutral phrase. |
| I will resolve at once your problem. | I'll take care of this **at once** for you. / I'll resolve your problem at once. | Do not put `at once` between the verb and its object. |
| I'll make sure that you will receive it. | I'll make sure that you **receive** it. | Use the present simple after `make sure that` for a future result. |

:::danger Spanish speakers
Do not write *I am sorry for the inconveniences* just because *inconvenientes* is plural in Spanish. Use the singular: `the inconvenience`. Also avoid *It is your fault* (*es su culpa*). It sounds rude in English.
:::

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

1. First of all, I'm terribly ______ about that.
2. If I ______ you correctly, the file did not arrive.
3. It looks like an ______ on our part.
4. I'll get ______ to you by Friday.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Make each sentence more polite and professional.

1. It is your mistake.
2. Tell me what happened.

### C. Speaking prompts

- Role-play an unhappy client call. Use at least one phrase from each of these steps: apologize, empathy, solution and follow-up.
- A client says, "Your app lost my data!" What do you say first?

## 8. Quick recap

- Start with an apology: `I apologize for ...`, `I'm terribly sorry about that.`
- Clarify and show empathy: `If I understand you correctly, ...`, `I can see why ...`.
- Take responsibility softly: `It looks like an oversight on our part.`
- Say how and when: `This should be resolved by ...`, `I'll get back to you ...`.
- End friendly: `Is there anything else I can help you with today?`

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] I can apologize with three different phrases.
- [ ] I can repeat the client's problem with `If I understand you correctly`.
- [ ] I can show empathy without blaming anyone.
- [ ] I can explain how and when I will fix a problem.
- [ ] I can offer an alternative and promise a follow-up.
- [ ] I can end the conversation in a friendly way.
