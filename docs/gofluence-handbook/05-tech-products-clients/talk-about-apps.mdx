# How to talk about apps

## 1. Learning objective

Describe what a desktop, mobile or web app does, compare its pros and cons, and explain its plans and prices.

## 2. Key concepts

You can talk about an app in three steps: **functions**, **pros and cons**, and **pricing**.

### Describe functions and features

```text title="Useful structures"
This ... app offers ...
... (also) helps / enables / allows ... to ...
... uses / makes use of ... to ...
... is (also) ..., especially for ...
... has ... features ...
```

- This project management app offers a clear task board.
- It also helps teams to track their work.
- The app makes use of AI to sort your emails.
- It is great, especially for remote teams.

:::tip Verb patterns
`allow` and `enable` need a person + `to`: *It allows users to share files.* `help` can use `to` or not: *It helps you save time.*
:::

### Discuss pros and cons

```text title="Useful structures"
On the plus side, ...
One of the downsides (of) ...
The disadvantage(s) (of ...) is / are balanced by ...
```

- On the plus side, the app works offline.
- One of the downsides of the free version is the limited storage.
- The disadvantage of the price is balanced by the great support.

### Talk about plans and pricing

```text title="Useful structures"
... starts / starting at ...
... costs ... per / a month / year, billed yearly / annually.
... will cost you ... per / a month / year per user.
```

- The Basic plan starts at 8 dollars a month, billed annually.
- The Team plan will cost you 12 dollars a month per user.

:::note Billed yearly
`billed annually` and `billed yearly` mean you pay once a year, not every month.
:::

:::warning `per` and `a`
Say `per month` or `a month`. Both are correct. Do not say *for month*.
:::

## 5. Common mistakes

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| It allows to share files. | It allows **users to** share files. | `allow` needs a person before `to`. |
| It costs 10 dollars for month. | It costs 10 dollars **per** month. | Use `per` or `a`. |
| On the plus side is the price low. | On the plus side, **the price is low**. | Normal word order after the phrase. |
| The disadvantage are balanced by the speed. | The disadvantage **is** balanced by the speed. | The verb agrees with `disadvantage`. |
| It starts in 8 dollars. | It starts **at** 8 dollars. | Use `at` with prices. |

:::danger Spanish speakers
Spanish says *Permite compartir archivos*. English needs the person: *It allows you to share files.* Also, `billed annually` is not *facturado anual*, so do not drop the `-ed`.
:::

## 6. Practice exercises

### A. Fill in the blanks

1. This app ______ us to share files with clients.
2. ______ the plus side, the app is free to start.
3. One of the ______ of this tool is the high price.
4. The Pro plan ______ at 20 dollars a month, billed annually.

### B. Rewrite the sentence

Rewrite each sentence with the phrase in brackets.

1. The app is good for freelancers. (especially for)
2. The app uses AI. It sorts your emails. (uses ... to)

### C. Speaking prompts

- Describe an app you use at work. What does it offer? Give one pro and one con.
- Explain the price of a plan you know. Use `starts at` and `billed annually`.

## 8. Quick recap

- Functions: `This app offers ...`, `It helps / enables / allows ... to ...`.
- Pros and cons: `On the plus side, ...`, `One of the downsides of ...`.
- Balance: `The disadvantage is balanced by ...`.
- Pricing: `starts at ...`, `costs ... per month, billed annually`.

## 9. Review checklist

- [ ] I can describe three functions of an app.
- [ ] I can give one pro and one con with the right phrases.
- [ ] I use `allow` and `enable` with a person + `to`.
- [ ] I can say a price with `per month` and `billed annually`.
- [ ] I can recommend an app aloud in 30 seconds.
