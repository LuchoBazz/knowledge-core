# Class Notes

Write your notes right after each class, while you still remember the details. Copy the template into a new section below (or into your own notes app).

:::tip Keep it short
Five minutes after class is enough. Focus on **mistakes** and **new words**. These are the most useful things to review.
:::

## Weekly review checklist

- [ ] I reread my mistakes from this week.
- [ ] I moved new words to my study list.
- [ ] I did the practice exercises of at least one topic.
- [ ] I said three new sentences out loud.
