# Common Mistakes for Spanish Speakers

Many errors come from translating word by word. Learn the pattern, not the translation.

## Grammar

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| If the team will finish, we celebrate. | If the team **finishes**, we celebrate. | No `will` after `if`. |
| She can to help you. | She can help you. | Modal + base verb, without `to`. |
| He cans speak English. | He **can** speak English. | Modals do not take `-s`. |
| I have visited the client yesterday. | I **visited** the client yesterday. | A finished time word needs the simple past. |
| It saves time by automate tasks. | It saves time by **automating** tasks. | After a preposition, use `-ing`. |
| Is a problem with the server. | **There is** a problem with the server. | English needs a subject: `there is` or `it is`. |
| Is raining. | **It is** raining. | Do not drop the subject. |
| I am agree with you. | I **agree** with you. | `agree` is a verb. Do not use `be`. |
| She must to leave. | She **must** leave. | No `to` after `must`. |
| He is more tall than me. | He is **taller** than me. | Short adjectives use `-er`. |

## Word choice and false friends

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| I am **actually** working on a new project. | I am **currently** working on a new project. | `actually` means `en realidad`, not `actualmente`. |
| I need to **assist** the meeting. | I need to **attend** the meeting. | `assist` = `ayudar`. `attend` = `asistir`. |
| We need more **informations**. | We need more **information**. | `information` is uncountable. No plural. |
| She gave me good **advices**. | She gave me good **advice**. | `advice` is uncountable. |
| I **realize** a report every week. | I **do** / **write** a report every week. | `realize` = `darse cuenta`, not `realizar`. |

## Prepositions and small words

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| I am interested **on** this project. | I am interested **in** this project. | Fixed pair: interested in. |
| We depend **of** the client. | We depend **on** the client. | Fixed pair: depend on. |
| I will arrive **to** the office at nine. | I will arrive **at** the office at nine. | `arrive at` a place, `arrive in` a city. |
| The meeting is **in** Monday. | The meeting is **on** Monday. | Days use `on`. |
