Prepositions of position

1. Learning objective​

Say where something is in an office, on a screen or in a document, using the main prepositions of position.

2. Key concepts​

A preposition of position shows the place of one thing in relation to another.

Structure
subject + be + preposition + noun
The printer is next to the kitchen.
PrepositionMeaningExample
insidewithin a spaceThe keys are inside the drawer.
outsidenot within a spaceThe team is waiting outside the meeting room.
above / overhigher than somethingThe clock is above the door.
belowlower than somethingPlease sign below the line.
on top oftouching the upper surfaceThe report is on top of the desk.
under / underneathlower, often coveredThe cables are under the desk.
next to / besideat the side, very closeMy desk is next to the window.
betweenin the middle of two thingsThe meeting room is between the kitchen and my office.
in the middle ofin the center of somethingThe table is in the middle of the room.
in front ofat the front, facingThe reception is in front of the elevator.
at the back of / behindat the rearThe storage room is at the back of the office.
Above vs over

above and over are often the same. Use over when something covers or crosses another thing (a lamp over the table). Use above for a simple higher level (the logo is above the title).

Under vs underneath

underneath is a little more emphatic than under. It often means something is hidden or covered.

Between is for two, among is for many

Use between for two things or people: between the printer and the door. For a group of three or more, use among: among the files.

  • On a screen

Your desk is next to the window.
The kitchen is between the meeting room and the restroom.
The storage room is at the back of the office.

Rob
💬 Finding the meeting room

Visitor: Excuse me, where is Room 3?

Maya: It is at the back of the office, behind the kitchen.

Visitor: Is it next to the elevator?

Maya: No, it is between the kitchen and the IT room.

5. Common mistakes​

❌ Wrong✅ CorrectWhy
The printer is in front the door.The printer is in front of the door.in front of needs of.
The room is between the kitchen.The room is between the kitchen and the office.between needs two items.
The folder is on the top the desk.The folder is on top of the desk.Use the fixed phrase on top of.
The keys are at the middle of the table.The keys are in the middle of the table.The fixed phrase is in the middle of.
It is in back the building.It is at the back of (or behind) the building.Use at the back of or behind.
Spanish speakers

Spanish uses one word for several ideas (encima, en, sobre). In English, on top of is for touching surfaces, above and over are higher without touching, and in and inside are for closed spaces. Also, do not forget of in in front of, on top of and in the middle of.

6. Practice exercises​

A. Fill in the blanks​

Use one preposition for each blank.

  1. The coffee machine is ______ the kitchen. (not in the kitchen)
  2. My laptop is ______ my bag. (within)
  3. The printer is ______ the two desks. (in the middle of two things)
  4. The logo is ______ the page title. (higher)

B. Rewrite the sentence​

Rewrite each sentence with the preposition in brackets.

  1. The keys are in the drawer. (inside)
  2. The manager sits in the back of the room. (at the back of)

C. Speaking prompts​

  • Describe the layout of your office or your home office. Use at least five prepositions.
  • Explain to a colleague where a button is on a screen you know well.

D. Quick quiz​

Choose the correct sentence.

7. Answer key​

Show answers

A.

  1. outside
  2. inside
  3. between
  4. above (or over)

B.

  1. The keys are inside the drawer.
  2. The manager sits at the back of the room.

C. Answers vary. Check that every sentence has be + preposition + noun and that you include of where needed.

8. Quick recap​

  • Prepositions of position show where one thing is in relation to another.
  • Main groups: inside / outside, above / below, on top of / under, next to / between, in front of / behind.
  • Fixed phrases keep of: on top of, in front of, in the middle of, at the back of.
  • between is for two things.

9. Review checklist​

  • I can name all eleven prepositions without looking.
  • I use of in in front of and on top of.
  • I can describe my workplace layout.
  • I can give directions to a meeting room.
  • I can explain where something is on a screen.
