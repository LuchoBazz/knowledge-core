How to discuss using AI tools at work

Assumptions

In the class notes, the heading "How to propose actions for project risks" appears just above this block. The content is about AI tools, so this page treats that block as the AI-tools lesson.

1. Learning objective​

Ask colleagues about the AI tools they use, ask follow-up questions, and describe what AI tools do in simple language.

2. Key concepts​

Ask for detailed information​

Questions
Do you use any AI tools in your team?
How does that help?
Do you use any other tools?

Start with a general question. Then ask a follow-up question to get details.

Talk about your habits​

StructureUseExample
I use AI to + verba specific taskI use AI to summarize long documents.
I use AI for + noun / -inga general purposeI use AI for brainstorming ideas.
AI helps me (to) + verba benefitAI helps me organize information faster.
I mainly use it when...when you usually use itI mainly use it when I write emails.

Describe what a tool does​

To describe a product in a simple way, use a present simple verb after It:

Structure
It checks / saves / gives / ... + object
  • It checks my grammar.
  • It saves me time.
  • It gives me ideas for titles.

Talk about results: It + verb + to / by + -ing​

Useful phrases
It makes it easier to ...
It allows us to ...
It can help us ...
It saves us time by + -ing
  • It makes it easier to understand complex topics.
  • It saves us time by automating repetitive tasks.
Always -ing after a preposition

After by, for and other prepositions, use the -ing form: by automating, for brainstorming.

to or by?

Use to after verbs like help, want and need. Use by to explain a method or action: It helps us by explaining new ideas.

4. Examples in a workplace context​

💬 Chat in the break room

Sam: Do you use any AI tools in your team?

Rosa: Yes. I use AI to summarize meeting notes.

Sam: How does that help?

Rosa: It saves me time by writing a short summary for me.

Sam: Do you use any other tools?

Rosa: I also use one for brainstorming ideas. It gives me many options.

  • Stand-up
  • Email
Stand-up update
I mainly use AI when I write test cases.
It checks my code and gives me ideas.
AI helps me find bugs more quickly.
Email to the team
Subject: AI tools in our team

Hi everyone,

I use AI for writing first drafts of reports.
It saves us time by automating repetitive tasks.
Do you use any other tools that help the team?

Thanks,
Julia

5. Common mistakes​

❌ Wrong✅ CorrectWhy
It saves us time by automate tasks.It saves us time by automating tasks.Use -ing after by.
It helps us to by learning AI.It helps us by learning about AI.Do not put to and by together.
I use AI for summarize documents.I use AI to summarize documents (or for summarizing).to + verb or for + -ing.
AI helps me to organizing ideas.AI helps me organize (or to organize) ideas.Base verb or to + verb.
It makes easier to write.It makes it easier to write.You need it after makes.
Spanish speakers

Spanish uses the infinitive after a preposition (por automatizar). English uses -ing: by automating. Also, do not say I use AI for to ....

6. Practice exercises​

A. Fill in the blanks​

  1. I use AI ______ (summarize) long documents.
  2. It saves us time by ______ (automate) repetitive tasks.
  3. I use AI for ______ (brainstorm) ideas.
  4. It makes ______ easier to understand complex topics.

B. Rewrite the sentence​

  1. I use AI for summarize reports. (correct it)
  2. AI organizes information. This helps me. (combine with helps me)

C. Speaking prompts​

  • Ask a colleague about their AI tools. Use one general question and two follow-ups.
  • Explain how you use AI at work. Use I use AI to ... and It saves me time by ....

D. Quick quiz​

Choose the correct sentence.

7. Answer key​

Show answers

A.

  1. to summarize
  2. automating
  3. brainstorming
  4. it

B.

  1. I use AI to summarize reports. (or I use AI for summarizing reports.)
  2. AI helps me organize information.

C. Answers vary. Check that you ask follow-ups such as How does that help? and use -ing after by.

8. Quick recap​

  • Ask: Do you use any AI tools in your team? then How does that help?
  • Habits: I use AI to + verb, I use AI for + noun / -ing, AI helps me (to) + verb.
  • Describe tools: It checks / saves / gives ....
  • Results: It makes it easier to ..., It saves us time by + -ing.

9. Review checklist​

  • I can ask a general question and a follow-up question.
  • I can use I use AI to and I use AI for correctly.
  • I always use -ing after by.
  • I can describe a tool with It checks / saves / gives.
  • I can explain how AI helps me at work.
